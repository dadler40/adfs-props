Scanned 1840 venue pairs across today's NFL games, NFL props and NBA points · 10:00 AM CT

An arb = the same bet priced so that buying YES on one site and NO on the other costs under 100¢ — the pair pays out 100¢ no matter what happens. Prices here are executable ones (what you'd actually pay: asks and bids, not midpoints). The sizing matters: a guaranteed profit needs equal shares on both sides, not equal dollars — the table shows the right split for $100 total, and what a flat $50/$50 would actually guarantee (sometimes nothing). Profits are gross, then after an estimated Kalshi trading fee; Polymarket sports charge no trading fee. Nothing here places a trade, and prices move — re-check both books before acting.
