Method: for every proposition both venues price, both directions are costed at executable prices (Kalshi ask / 100 − bid; Polymarket best ask / 100 − best bid). Guaranteed profit assumes equal shares on both legs and settlement at 100¢. Kalshi fee estimated at the standard 0.07 × P × (1−P) per contract on the Kalshi leg — the actual fee varies by series. Depth is checked at the quoted prices — when fewer shares are fillable than the $100 split needs, the row says so. All Polymarket prices here are off the Polymarket US book (the only one Daniel can trade) — screen quotes come from the US gateway and every arb shown above was re-checked against the live books first (59 screen arbs checked, 2 survived). An edge far bigger than a few cents usually means one quote is stale, not free money — verify both books live before acting. Page republishes on the board's refresh cycle.
