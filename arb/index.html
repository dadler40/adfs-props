Arbitrage — Kalshi vs Polymarket

Scanned 702 venue pairs across today's NFL games, NFL props and NBA points · 6:19 PM CT

An arb = the same bet priced so that buying YES on one site and NO on the other costs under 100¢ — the pair pays out 100¢ no matter what happens. Prices here are executable ones (what you'd actually pay: asks and bids, not midpoints). The sizing matters: a guaranteed profit needs equal shares on both sides, not equal dollars — the table shows the right split for $100 total, and both endings of a flat $50/$50 — which often guarantees nothing, because $50 buys different share counts at different prices. Profits are gross, then after an estimated Kalshi trading fee; Polymarket sports charge no trading fee. Nothing here places a trade, and prices move — re-check both books before acting.

Method: for every proposition both venues price, both directions are costed at executable prices (Kalshi ask / 100 − bid; Polymarket best ask / 100 − best bid). Guaranteed profit assumes equal shares on both legs and settlement at 100¢. Kalshi fee estimated at the standard 0.07 × P × (1−P) per contract on the Kalshi leg — the actual fee varies by series. Depth is checked at the quoted prices — when fewer shares are fillable than the $100 split needs, the row says so. All Polymarket prices here are off the Polymarket US book (the only one Daniel can trade) — screen quotes come from the US gateway and every arb shown above was re-checked against the live books first (66 screen arbs checked, 0 survived). An edge far bigger than a few cents usually means one quote is stale, not free money — verify both books live before acting. Page republishes on the board's refresh cycle.
