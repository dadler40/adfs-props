what's a vintage?
PILOT — unproven. These are model suggestions from a graded experiment, not a proven edge. Every row is graded the next morning (hit rate, CLV, per-stat accuracy); the track record is what earns trust.

Rolling record

Confidence (0–100): pilot model score — the probability implied by the forecast's difference vs the graded per-market error, widened when the three estimators disagree, capped at 85 until calibrated, and capped at 59 when a row depends on an unresolved injury scenario. Not yet a calibrated hit rate.

ETR now (Δ exp): on pre-final issues the Forecast is the best guess at the FINAL ETR number. This column shows the current ETR projection and the total expected move into the final set — ETR's morning→final drift plus a per-player adjustment when the day's injury news implies one. At the pre-lock issue the final set is in and the column disappears.

Rec. Conf (recommended confidence): the hit rate of graded rows issued in the same market at the same model-confidence band, shrunk toward 50% — it is what the graded record says a score like this has actually been worth. Appears once a band has 10+ graded rows, and sharpens as more slates grade out.

Matchup Score (0–100): 60% game environment (both teams' total projected fantasy points, ranked against today's other games) + 40% the player's share of his own team's projection. Higher = better environment for his number.

Difference: our forecast minus the site's line, in points (absolute). Agree: whether all three estimators (A/B/C) land on the same side of the line.
